# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS

## Users

Thumbtack pros on their phones, running their business between jobs — responding to leads, managing their schedule and profile, and checking on money. They move through the app in short bursts, often one-handed and outdoors.

## Product Purpose

A navigation concept for the Thumbtack pro app. It exists to test a new navigation structure end to end as a prototype, so the structure can be judged in use rather than on a static canvas. Success is a nav engineering can build from.

## Positioning

The structure is the artifact. This is not a feature prototype with navigation attached; the navigation is what is being designed, and every screen behind it exists to prove the structure holds.

## Operating Context

Pros use this on a phone. The prototype is built at 390 wide and previewed in a device frame, in both light and dark mode.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML and CSS, no framework and no build step. Pushpin's token stylesheet is the only styling dependency.
- Fidelity is `handoff`: the code ends at a Figma frame for engineering handoff, not in production.
- The navigation structure itself is open — which sections exist and what they are called is a design decision, not a fact to preserve.

## Brand Commitments

Pushpin is this project's design system: its tokens, component catalog, and copy rules bind every surface. Thumbtack's voice governs the words.

## Evidence on Hand

None. There is no real pro content, data, or screenshot set in this project. Lead names, job titles, prices, dates, and ratings in the prototype are placeholders and must not be presented as real Thumbtack data.

## Product Principles

- The navigation is the subject; screens serve it.
- Judge it in a phone frame in motion, not as a flat comp.
- Built for one-handed use in short bursts between jobs.
- Pushpin's tokens and components decide the surface; nothing is hand-rolled that the system already publishes.
- Placeholder content stays obviously placeholder.
